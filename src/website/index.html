👋 About Me

I'm a student and programmer who spends far too much time making things that probably could have been solved with an existing program.

I've been messing around with computers and programming for years, and these days most of my free time ends up somewhere between writing code, breaking code, fixing code, and wondering why the code worked in the first place.

I especially enjoy low-level programming, Linux, game development, and building things from scratch just to see whether I can.

💻 Computers Are Kinda My Thing

My main playground is Linux, especially Arch Linux. I like understanding how things actually work rather than just clicking a button and hoping for the best.

My favorite language is Lua, and I spend a lot of time with LÖVE2D. I also work with C, C++, Python, and JavaScript depending on what kind of questionable idea has entered my brain that day.
